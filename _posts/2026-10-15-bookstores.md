Inspired by my friend Shadab's musings on [what he would do in London](https://dufferzafar.com/2021/what-would-i-do-in-london/), I decided to put together a list of bookstores I have visited and purchased from around the world. I compiled this retrospectively in Oct 2026 and will update it regularly.

**Delhi**

1. All the second-hand book sellers, NFC
2. [Faqir Chand & Sons](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Faqir+Chand+%26+Sons%2C+Khan+Market%2C+New+Delhi), Khan Market
3. [Bahrisons](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bahrisons+Booksellers%2C+Khan+Market%2C+New+Delhi), Khan Market
4. Delhi Book Fair, Pragati Maidan
5. International Book Fair, Pragati Maidan

**Bangalore**

1. [Atta Galatta](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Atta+Galatta%2C+Koramangala%2C+Bengaluru), Koramangala
2. [Landmark](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nexus+Mall+%28formerly+Forum+Mall%29%2C+Koramangala%2C+Bengaluru), Forum Mall, Koramangala
3. [Crossword](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Crossword+Bookstore%2C+Nexus+Whitefield+%28formerly+Forum+Neighbourhood+Mall%29%2C+Whitefield+Main+Road%2C+Bengaluru), Forum Neighbourhood Mall, Whitefield
